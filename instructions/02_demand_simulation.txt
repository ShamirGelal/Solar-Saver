SOLAR SAVER AGENT ASSIGNMENT: SYNTHETIC CITY DEMAND

MISSION
Create a credible, deterministic synthetic Charlottesville workload generator.
Your component supplies the scheduler with fixed demand, flexible workloads,
solar supply, and capacity limits. It does not decide when workloads run.

READ FIRST
- README.md
- solar_saver/domain.py
- solar_saver/contracts.py
- solar_saver/demand.py
- tests/test_contracts.py

FILES YOU OWN
- solar_saver/demand.py
- tests/test_demand.py (create this)
- data/demo/* if a small documented demo fixture is helpful

FILES YOU MUST NOT CHANGE WITHOUT TEAM AGREEMENT
- solar_saver/domain.py
- solar_saver/contracts.py
- solar_saver/forecasting/*
- solar_saver/scheduling.py
- solar_saver/metrics.py
- solar_saver/pipeline.py

REQUIRED PUBLIC CONTRACT
Maintain:

    generate(
        solar_profile: SolarProfile,
        config: SimulationConfig,
    ) -> CityScenario

The same random seed and inputs must produce the same CityScenario.

MVP REQUIREMENTS
- Exactly 24 hourly slots.
- A fixed background-demand profile.
- At least four flexible workload categories.
- Each workload has power, duration, allowed window, preferred time, priority,
  and flexibility.
- Every workload must fit inside its own allowed window.
- Generated assumptions must be clearly documented as synthetic.

RECOMMENDED CATEGORIES
- EV charging
- Laundry
- Dishwasher
- Battery charging
- Water heating

Use realistic-looking ranges, but do not describe synthetic assumptions as
measured Charlottesville behavior. Keep critical infrastructure out of the MVP
unless the scheduler team has agreed on hard safety constraints.

ACCEPTANCE TESTS
- Same seed gives identical scenarios.
- Different seeds change the workload population.
- Every profile has 24 finite, nonnegative values.
- Every workload has a unique ID.
- Every workload fits within earliest_start and latest_finish.
- Preferred starts lie inside valid windows.
- Total requested flexible energy can be calculated exactly.
- Existing tests/test_contracts.py continues to pass.

DELIVERABLE FOR INTEGRATION
Tell the integration owner:
- workload categories and assumptions;
- typical scenario size;
- expected runtime for 100 and 500 users;
- any proposed domain-contract change before making it.

DO NOT BUILD
- Solar prediction
- Schedule placement
- Schedule metrics
- UI components
- Claims about real Charlottesville demand
