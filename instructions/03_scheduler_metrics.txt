SOLAR SAVER AGENT ASSIGNMENT: SCHEDULING AND METRICS

MISSION
Implement and validate the algorithms that place flexible workloads. Compare an
uncoordinated baseline, a solar-only policy, and a balanced policy. The balanced
policy must consider solar utilization, capacity, user preference, priority, and
fairness without changing total requested workload energy.

REQUIRED PUBLIC CONTRACT
Maintain:

    schedule(
        scenario: CityScenario,
        strategy: Strategy,
        weights: SchedulerWeights,
    ) -> ScheduleResult

All aggregate arrays must match scenario.horizon_hours.

STRATEGIES
UNCOORDINATED:
- Place each task as close to its preferred time as possible.
- It may expose capacity violations; those violations are a baseline metric.

SOLAR_ONLY:
- Reduce incremental grid draw while obeying workload windows and capacity.
- Ignore preference except for deterministic tie-breaking.

BALANCED:
- Reduce incremental grid draw.
- Penalize preference deviation.
- Penalize approaching the capacity limit.
- Penalize large individual deviations more than several small deviations.
- Give important workloads more protection from displacement.

The existing greedy scheduler is a working reference, not a final optimizer.
Improve it only after its invariants are protected by tests. A mixed-integer
optimizer is optional, not required.

REQUIRED METRICS
- grid energy used;
- solar energy used;
- unused solar energy;
- renewable matching percentage;
- peak demand;
- average and maximum preference deviation;
- overall completion percentage;
- critical completion percentage;
- capacity-violation hours.

ACCEPTANCE TESTS
- A scheduled task appears no more than once.
- Assignments remain inside allowed windows.
- Assignment duration matches workload duration.
- Balanced and solar-only schedules obey capacity.
- Scheduled flexible energy is conserved for all completed workloads.
- Metrics recompute correctly from a small hand-calculated scenario.
- Algorithms are deterministic.
- Existing tests/test_contracts.py continues to pass.
