    HOW TO READ THIS DEMO

    Better timing, visible trade-offs.

    Uncoordinated scheduling follows each household's preferred time and may exceed capacity. Solar-only shifts requests toward available solar within their allowed windows and capacity. Balanced also considers preference, priority, peak demand, and fairness. These are deterministic greedy policies, not guarantees of a globally optimal schedule.
